From his childhood, Paisios felt a calling, not only to the isolated life of a monk, but to the 'Hesychastic' life of complete isolation desert. But the journey was long, and the needs of the people around him pulled him back into the world over and over again.

Conscripted into the Greek army, who were currently supressing guerilla rebels after WWII, Paisios prayed that he would not be forced to harm another. Miraculously, he was assigned to be a radio technician.
